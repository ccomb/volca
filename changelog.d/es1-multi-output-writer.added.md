- The EcoSpold 1 export writes a block of several products kept whole as one
  dataset with its allocation keys: one per product, at its share scaled to
  100 %, weighed by the prices the products state and failing that by the
  shares they declare, which the export says it replaces. Read back, the
  block divides by those shares, and its buyers are linked to it again. A
  block nothing weighs is written without keys, and the export says why; an
  input buying one of its coproducts is refused.
- A coproduct of a block kept whole has a number of its own in that block,
  which its buyers quote: a dataset buying two products of one block states
  two numbers, and two blocks making one product are told apart. Reading
  EcoSpold 1, such a number links the input to the process making the
  product, the block kept whole or the piece cut from it.
- One flow stated on several lines of a dataset and bought from one supplier
  is exported as one line of their total, and the export says so.
