- Wire revision 118. The consumers of an upstream process can be asked under parameter values and swaps, by a POST to the `supply-chain/consumers` route with the same body as the supply-chain POST. They are then the consumers the swapped supply chain links it to: the new supplier lists the processes a swap put it under, and the swapped-out one no longer lists them. The lines shown stay the database's own: what a swap moved onto a supplier is in the consumer's part but on no line, so a link made by a swap shows no line, and a consumer that already bought the new supplier shows a part larger than its lines.
