- The `supply-chain/consumers` route says, for one process the supply chain
  lists, which processes of that chain consume it, how much of its quantity
  each one accounts for, and the lines it is bought on, with their comments.
  Soy beans listed at 0.09 kg under a chicken can be read as 0.06 kg bought by
  a compound feed and 0.03 kg by a soybean meal. What consumers in a database
  whose licence keeps its amounts account for is given as unexplained, with the
  database named.
