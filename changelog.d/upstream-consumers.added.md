- The `supply-chain/consumers` route says, for one process the supply chain
  lists, which processes of that chain consume it, how much of its quantity
  each one accounts for, and the lines it is bought on, with their comments.
  Read at the same `min-quantity` as the supply chain, it lists the consumers
  the row counts. What the consumers under that minimum, or in a database whose
  licence keeps its amounts, account for is given as unexplained, with those
  databases named.
