- Wire revision 117. The path to a supplier can be asked under parameter values and swaps, by a POST to the path-to route with the same body as the supply-chain POST. Its steps then follow the swapped chain: the new supplier can be the target, and every quantity is the one the swapped supply chain lists. The MCP `get_path_to` tool takes the same `substitutions` and `parameters` as `get_supply_chain`.
